What changed, and why

A companion note to the draft site · 5 August 2026

Nothing in your copy has been changed. Not a sentence, not a heading, not a claim. Every word on the draft is the word already on hhglobalholdings.com. What has changed is what the page does with it.

The draft is not live and cannot be found. It sits on BridgePointAI's site, marked noindex, blocked in robots.txt, with a bar across the bottom stating it is a concept build.

The idea behind all of it

HH's central argument is that a claim should carry its own evidence. That argument is made in prose across the site, and it is a good argument. The draft tries to make the page behave that way rather than only say it.

So every moving thing on the draft explains something. Nothing moves for decoration.

What was fixed underneath

There is also a small malformed CSS rule inherited from the source, .hero-bg {{ with a doubled brace and no matching element. It changes nothing visually and is repaired in the draft.

What was deliberately not done

No AI-generated photography. A generated image of a laboratory that is not your laboratory, on a site whose argument is that claims should be verifiable, is a bad trade. Every photograph on the draft is yours. The only generated imagery is abstract texture, which makes no factual claim.

No scroll hijacking. The usual shortcut to a "premium" feel is to take over the scroll wheel with inertia. It breaks trackpads, harms accessibility and is increasingly marked down. Fluidity here comes from scroll-driven animation instead, which is native and smooth.

No new claims. Nothing on the draft asserts an accreditation, a certification or a capability that is not already claimed on the live site.

Accessibility and restraint

  • Every animation stops for anyone whose system requests reduced motion. The chain of custody becomes a plain, fully visible list with the chain shown as verified.
  • All diagrams carry text descriptions for screen readers.
  • No horizontal overflow at any width tested, from 390px to 1440px.
  • The page loads in about two thirds of a second.

The one thing that would improve this most

Ten seconds of Lab 24, filmed on a phone. Silent, landscape, locked off, no panning. A sequencing run underway, a plate being loaded, or the clean room airlock cycling.

The band is already built and waiting. Drop the file in and it plays. It would be the one thing on the page that no competitor could copy, because it is simply true.
